# Nike vs.Addidas: Footwear Revenue
For my Choose Your Own Hypothesis project, I want to compare the footwear sales revenue of Nike and Adidas to understand how their sales performance has changed over time. I plan to collect original financial data from the companies' official annual reports. My main focus is on annual footwear revenue and year-over-year revenue growth. I will use exploratory data analysis to visualize the trends and investigate whether the two brands have different average revenue growth rates. If the data supports the statistical assumptions, I will use a permutation test and bootstrapping to estimate uncertainty. I will also consider differences in currency, reporting periods, and company size when interpreting the results.
